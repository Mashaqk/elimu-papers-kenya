Mission

ELIMUPEPE is a Kenyan digital learning platform that provides high-quality examinations, assignments, revision papers, marking schemes, and learning resources for learners from Grade 7 to Grade 12. The platform enables learners, parents, and teachers to purchase individual educational resources at affordable prices without requiring monthly or yearly subscriptions.

Professional Design Direction

The website should have a modern, clean, and trustworthy appearance inspired by leading educational platforms while reflecting the Kenyan context.

Primary Color: Royal Blue (#1E3A8A)

Secondary Color: Emerald Green (#16A34A)

Accent Color: Golden Yellow (#FACC15)

Background: White (#FFFFFF) with Light Gray (#F8FAFC) sections

Typography: Poppins for headings and Inter for body text

Layout: Spacious with rounded cards, soft shadows, and smooth hover animations

Responsive Design: Mobile-first, ensuring an excellent experience for smartphone users across Kenya

This structure keeps ELIMUPEPE simple to navigate, affordable through one-time purchases, and tailored specifically to learners in Grades 7–12.
